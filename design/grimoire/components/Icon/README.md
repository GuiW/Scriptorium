Grimoire business icons, taken from Lucide (ISC licence) and drawn with a 1.5px stroke; they inherit the text colour (`currentColor`).

- `name`: one of the business names below (not the Lucide name); `size` in px (20 by default; 16 in a line of text, 24 in desktop navigation; `BookmarkTabs` brings them down to 16); `label` for a lone icon that carries meaning (otherwise it is hidden from screen readers: pair it with a word).
- Colour: `ink-muted` at rest, `ink` on hover, otherwise the colour of its context (`on-crimson` text in an active bookmark, `gold-ink` for a reward). Never a `gold` icon on parchment.
- Mapping: `quete` (scroll-text), `journal` (book-open), `session` (calendar-days), `pnj` (user-round), `faction` (shield), `joueurs` (users), `mj` (venetian-mask), `lieu` (map-pin), `carte` (map), `butin` (gem), `pieces` (coins), `xp` (sparkles), `rumeur` (ear), `indice` (key), `secret` (eye-off), `urgent` (flame), `danger` (skull), `reglages` (settings), `ajouter` (plus), `rechercher` (search), `filtrer` (funnel, formerly filter), `plus` (ellipsis).
- Quest statuses keep their glyphs (◆ ✓ ✕ ?) and Secret MJ its broken seal: they are signs of the system, not icons.
- To add an icon: take it from Lucide, set it to 1.5px, give it a French business name (like the existing ones), and add it here and to the « Icons » asset group.
